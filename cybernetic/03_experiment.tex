\section{Research Design}
\begin{frame}[t]{A 2 $\times$ 2 Field Experiment}
\small
P\&G professionals joined one-day workshops between May and July 2024. Random assignment varied human teamwork and AI access.

\vspace{0.42cm}
\renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.65}
\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{@{}>{\bfseries}lXX@{}}\toprule
&\textbf{Without AI}&\textbf{With AI}\\\midrule
Individual&154 people&155 people\\
Two-person team&115 pairs / 230 people&126 pairs / 252 people\\\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}

\vspace{0.45cm}
Each team paired one R\&D employee with one commercial employee. An individual or pair submitted one final solution.

\vspace{0.33cm}
\begin{alertblock}{Analytical sample}
\textbf{791 participants} produced \textbf{550 submissions} for expert assessment.
\end{alertblock}
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}[t]{Experimental Workflow}
\small
\vspace{0.30cm}
\begin{columns}[T,onlytextwidth]
\begin{column}{.31\textwidth}
{\Large\textcolor{paperred}{\bfseries 1}}\par\medskip
{\bfseries Prepare}\par\smallskip
Participants receive task guidance and a condition. AI groups learn to use a GPT-4-based tool.
\end{column}
\begin{column}{.31\textwidth}
{\Large\textcolor{paperred}{\bfseries 2}}\par\medskip
{\bfseries Create}\par\smallskip
Each individual or pair generates five ideas, chooses one and develops a final proposal.
\end{column}
\begin{column}{.31\textwidth}
{\Large\textcolor{paperred}{\bfseries 3}}\par\medskip
{\bfseries Evaluate}\par\smallskip
Experts rate the proposal. Surveys record emotional change; interaction data show AI use.
\end{column}
\end{columns}

\vspace{0.82cm}
\begin{block}{Why the sequence matters}
Generating good options and selecting the best one are separate parts of the innovation process.
\end{block}
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}[t]{Measures and Estimation}
\small
\begin{columns}[T,onlytextwidth]
\begin{column}{.49\textwidth}
\textcolor{paperred}{\bfseries Outcomes}\par\smallskip
\textbf{Quality:} expert ratings (1--10), standardized for comparison.\par\smallskip
\textbf{Technicality:} commercial (1) to technical (7).\par\smallskip
\textbf{Emotions:} change from pretask to post-task survey.
\end{column}
\begin{column}{.47\textwidth}
\textcolor{paperred}{\bfseries Comparison}\par\smallskip
Each treatment coefficient compares with individuals working without AI. Additional models account for business unit, workshop date, employee characteristics and prior AI use.
\end{column}
\end{columns}

\vspace{0.55cm}
\begin{block}{Estimation}
\centering
{\footnotesize $Y_i=\beta_0+\beta_1\mathrm{TeamNoAI}_i+\beta_2\mathrm{AloneAI}_i+\beta_3\mathrm{TeamAI}_i+\gamma X_i+\delta FE_i+\varepsilon_i$}\par\smallskip
\raggedright The three $\beta$ terms capture the effects of human teamwork, AI-assisted individual work and AI-assisted teamwork relative to the control group.
\end{block}
\end{frame}
